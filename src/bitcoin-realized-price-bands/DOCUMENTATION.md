# BITCOIN REALIZED PRICE BANDS

## 📝 Overview

The **CandelaCharts - Bitcoin Realized Price Bands** indicator maps out key valuation zones for Bitcoin by comparing its current price against its Realized Price. By defining multiple bands above and below the Realized Price, this tool helps long-term investors and macro traders identify periods of extreme undervaluation (buying opportunities) and extreme overvaluation (taking profits).

The indicator displays these zones directly on the main price chart and includes a normalized MVRV (Market-Value-to-Realized-Value) ratio oscillator for a clearer view of historical extremes.

## 📦 Features

- **Realized Price Calculation:** Automatically fetches Bitcoin's Realized Market Cap and Divides it by Supply to calculate the true Realized Price.
- **Dynamic Valuation Bands:** Plots bands representing different multiples of the Realized Price (Lower Band, Mid Line, Overvalued, Extreme Bubble).
- **MVRV Oscillator:** Displays an oscillator representing the ratio of Current Price to Realized Price, highlighting cyclical tops and bottoms.
- **Automated Signals:** Generates clear Long and Short signals when the oscillator crosses key fundamental thresholds.
- **Candle Coloring:** Visually colors candles based on Bitcoin's valuation state (e.g., Undervalued, Neutral, Overvalued).
- **Background Highlights:** Emphasizes deep value zones and bubble territories directly on the chart background.

## ⚙️ Settings

- **Text Size / Font:** Customize the global text style and size for the indicator's brand watermark.
- **Hide Brand:** Toggle the visibility of the CandelaCharts watermark.
- **Lower Band (Default: 0.75):** Multiplier for the extreme undervaluation band.
- **Realized Price (Default: 1.0):** The baseline Realized Price level. Represents the average cost basis of all circulating coins.
- **Mid Line (Default: 1.5):** Multiplier representing a neutral or transitional valuation.
- **Overvalued (Default: 2.5):** Multiplier representing the beginning of an overvalued market state.
- **Extreme Bubble (Default: 3.5):** Multiplier denoting historic cyclical top territories.
- **Band Colors:** Customize the colors for Positive (Undervalued), Negative (Overvalued), and Neutral zones.
- **Show Main Chart Visuals:** Toggle the visibility of all indicators overlayed on the main price chart.
- **Show Background Highlight:** Toggle the background color shading for extreme valuation zones.

## 🌟 Benefits

- **Macro Perspective:** Strip away short-term noise and evaluate Bitcoin's price relative to its fundamental on-chain cost basis.
- **Clear Action Zones:** Easily spot historical accumulation zones (when price is near or below the Realized Price) and distribution zones (when price reaches extreme multiples).
- **Visual Clarity:** Gradients and clean fills in both the main chart and oscillator provide an immediate understanding of current market conditions.
- **Actionable Signals:** Built-in crossover signals help pinpoint potential macro reversals.

## 📒 Usage

- Use the Lower Band and Realized Price levels to identify historic accumulation zones for long-term spot positions.
- Monitor the Overvalued and Extreme Bubble levels to gauge when the market is overheated and consider taking profits.
- Combine the built-in crossover signals with broader market context to time macro entries and exits.
