# GLOBAL M2

## 📝 Overview

The **CandelaCharts - Global M2** indicator tracks and aggregates the global money supply (M2) across multiple major economies, converting all values into USD for comparability. It provides a macroeconomic perspective on liquidity conditions worldwide, displaying both the total M2 (in trillions USD) and its year-over-year growth rate.

## 📦 Features

- Aggregates M2 data from **19 countries and regions**, including the USA, Eurozone, China, Japan, UK, Canada, Australia, India, South Korea, Brazil, Russia, Switzerland, Mexico, Indonesia, Turkey, Saudi Arabia, Argentina, South Africa, and Qatar.
- Displays **total global M2** in trillions of USD.
- Calculates and shows **Year-over-Year (YoY) growth rate**.
- Optional **Moving Average (SMA, EMA, WMA)** smoothing on Global M2.
- Configurable **time offset** in weeks (forward/backward).
- Clean **data label table** showing latest M2 and YoY values.
- Auto-detection of chart background for adaptive styling.
- Branding toggle for minimal or full display.

## ⚙️ Settings

- **Moving Average**
  - Enable/Disable: Toggle smoothing
  - Type: SMA, EMA, WMA
  - Length: Default 200 (user configurable)
  - Color: Customizable
- **M2 Options**
  - Offset: Shift plot by ±52 weeks
  - Line Color: Customizable
  - Country Toggles: Enable or disable individual regions’ M2 data

## 📒 Usage

The **CandelaCharts - Global M2** is designed for macro-oriented traders and investors. It helps visualize how global liquidity dynamics affect financial markets.

- Use the **Global M2 line** to track the expansion or contraction of worldwide money supply, a key driver of market risk appetite.
- Compare **YoY growth rates** to inflation cycles: strong positive growth often coincides with bull markets, while contraction may foreshadow bear phases.
- Enable the **Moving Average** to identify long-term liquidity trends and smooth out short-term fluctuations.
- Apply **offsets** to synchronize liquidity shifts with specific market cycles or leading/lagging effects.
- Combine Global M2 with **major stock indices, crypto, or commodities** to see how liquidity supports or undermines price action.
- Monitor changes in **specific regions** (e.g., US, China, Eurozone) by toggling them on/off to isolate regional contributions to global liquidity.
- Use the indicator as a **macro confluence tool** with interest rates, CPI, credit spreads, and central bank policies.
- Traders can integrate this into **risk management frameworks** to assess when liquidity conditions favor aggressive or defensive positioning.

## 🚨 Alerts

Currently, the script does not include built-in alerts. Traders can manually set alerts on the plotted **Global M2 line** or **Moving Average** for threshold crossings or trend breaks.
