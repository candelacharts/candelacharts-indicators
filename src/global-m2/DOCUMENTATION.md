# GLOBAL M2

 📝 [b]Overview[/b]

The [b]CandelaCharts - Global M2[/b] indicator tracks and aggregates the global money supply (M2) across multiple major economies, converting all values into USD for comparability. It provides a macroeconomic perspective on liquidity conditions worldwide, displaying both the total M2 (in trillions USD) and its year-over-year growth rate.

 📦 [b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*] Aggregates M2 data from [b]19 countries and regions[/b], including the USA, Eurozone, China, Japan, UK, Canada, Australia, India, South Korea, Brazil, Russia, Switzerland, Mexico, Indonesia, Turkey, Saudi Arabia, Argentina, South Africa, and Qatar.
[*] Displays [b]total global M2[/b] in trillions of USD.
[*] Calculates and shows [b]Year-over-Year (YoY) growth rate[/b].
[*] Optional [b]Moving Average (SMA, EMA, WMA)[/b] smoothing on Global M2.
[*] Configurable [b]time offset[/b] in weeks (forward/backward).
[*] Clean [b]data label table[/b] showing latest M2 and YoY values.
[*] Auto-detection of chart background for adaptive styling.
[*] Branding toggle for minimal or full display.
[/list]

 ⚙️ [b]Settings[/b]

 📒 [b]Usage[/b]

The [b]CandelaCharts - Global M2[/b] is designed for macro-oriented traders and investors. It helps visualize how global liquidity dynamics affect financial markets.

[list]
[*] Use the [b]Global M2 line[/b] to track the expansion or contraction of worldwide money supply, a key driver of market risk appetite.
[*] Compare [b]YoY growth rates[/b] to inflation cycles: strong positive growth often coincides with bull markets, while contraction may foreshadow bear phases.
[*] Enable the [b]Moving Average[/b] to identify long-term liquidity trends and smooth out short-term fluctuations.
[*] Apply [b]offsets[/b] to synchronize liquidity shifts with specific market cycles or leading/lagging effects.
[*] Combine Global M2 with [b]major stock indices, crypto, or commodities[/b] to see how liquidity supports or undermines price action.
[*] Monitor changes in [b]specific regions[/b] (e.g., US, China, Eurozone) by toggling them on/off to isolate regional contributions to global liquidity.
[*] Use the indicator as a [b]macro confluence tool[/b] with interest rates, CPI, credit spreads, and central bank policies.
[*] Traders can integrate this into [b]risk management frameworks[/b] to assess when liquidity conditions favor aggressive or defensive positioning.
[/list]

 🚨 [b]Alerts[/b]

Currently, the script does not include built-in alerts. Traders can manually set alerts on the plotted [b]Global M2 line[/b] or [b]Moving Average[/b] for threshold crossings or trend breaks.
